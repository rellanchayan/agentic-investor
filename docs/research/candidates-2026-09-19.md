## Candidates researched and rejected this week

**AMZN (Amazon):** AWS is a genuine wide-moat business, but Amazon's trailing-twelve-month free cash flow turned negative in 2026 — the company is spending at a reported $200B annual capex rate on AI infrastructure. A company with negative FCF fails our "real leftover cash" standard. Watch for when the capex cycle peaks; it is not ready for a thesis today. Source: [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/aws-is-booming-but-amazons-free-cash-flow-turns-negative-on-record-ai-spending/), [MindStudio analysis](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/aws-free-cash-flow-collapsed-26b-to-1-2b-ai-infrastructure-spending).

**META (Meta Platforms):** FCF dropped 91% year-over-year to ~$784M in Q2 2026 on massive AI data-center capex. Operating income fell while revenue grew. EPS missed by a wide margin. Same capex problem as AMZN. Not ready. Source: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/meta-q2-earnings-report-2026.html), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/30/zuckerberg-superintelligence-meta-cash-flow-drop/).

**SHW (Sherwin-Williams):** Quality distribution moat (10,000+ owned Paint Stores, professional contractor loyalty) and strong revenue growth (+7.5% Q2 2026), but carries $13.8B total debt / ~$11.5B net debt against ~$4.5B EBITDA — net debt/EBITDA at 3.45x, which is above the company's own stated target of 2-2.5x. The balance sheet does not yet meet our "healthy, not drowning in debt" standard. Worth re-evaluating once leverage falls below 2.5x. Source: [Investing.com Q2 2026 results](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/sherwinwilliams-q2-2026-slides-earnings-beat-guidance-raised-93CH-4817539), [GuruFocus debt data](https://www.gurufocus.com/term/debt-to-ebitda/SHW).

### 1. SPGI — S&P Global Inc.

**One line on the business:** S&P Global provides credit ratings (the #1 ratings agency globally), financial indices (the S&P 500 index itself), market intelligence data, and commodity price benchmarks (Platts) — all of which counterparties are effectively required to use.

**One line on the moat:** The ratings business is a regulated duopoly with Moody's. Issuers must pay for a rating to access capital markets; investors must use those ratings to comply with mandates. The indices and data businesses sit on top of the same structural necessity. Gurufocus rates the moat at 8/10. Source: [GuruFocus moat analysis](https://www.gurufocus.com/term/moat-score/SPGI).

**Smell test:**
- Large and liquid: market cap ~$120-130B, NYSE-listed. Passes.
- Profitable: Q1 2026 revenue $4.17B (+10% YoY), net income $1.395B, adjusted EPS $4.97 (+13.7% YoY). Passes.
- Positive FCF: Yes — the ratings and data businesses are asset-light with minimal capex requirements. (Full FCF number to be confirmed in thesis.)
- Balance sheet: Asset-light model, no heavy debt load needed. Passes on face.
- Durable demand: Credit markets, financial benchmarks, and commodity data will exist in 10 years. Passes.

**Valuation attractiveness (not a thesis — just a smell test):** SPGI's trailing PE of ~24.8x is 20% below its 10-year median of 30.95x. Forward PE is ~23x. For a business growing revenue 10% and EPS 14%, this looks potentially fairly valued to moderately attractive. The analyst should build a proper DCF. Source: [Yahoo Finance Q1 2026 earnings](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/p-global-q1-earnings-revenues-173000735.html), [SPGI fundamental analysis](https://www.worldreview1989.com/2026/09/SP-Global-Inc-Stock-Analysis.html).

**Sector gap filled:** Financial information and data services — different from the transactional-finance names (V, MA, JPM) already on the watchlist.

### 2. ISRG — Intuitive Surgical Inc.

**One line on the business:** Intuitive makes the da Vinci robotic surgical system and sells the instruments, accessories, and services that hospitals must buy to keep using it — a classic installed-base "razor and blade" model.

**One line on the moat:** Intuitive has ~85% global market share in robotic-assisted soft-tissue surgery. Switching costs are compounded by the decades-long investment in surgeon training programs (SimNow simulation, fellowships, on-site proctoring). Every surgeon trained on da Vinci is a durable lock-in for that hospital. Source: [Intuitive Q1 2026 earnings release](https://isrg.intuitive.com/news-releases/news-release-details/intuitive-announces-first-quarter-earnings-7), [SubStack ISRG analysis](https://denisdoroshenko.substack.com/p/intuitive-surgical-isrg-executive).

**Smell test:**
- Large and liquid: market cap ~$138.9B. Passes.
- Profitable: Q1 2026 revenue $2.77B (+23% YoY). EPS consensus implies ~20% growth for FY2026. Passes.
- Positive FCF: TTM FCF $2.83B; H1 2026 FCF jumped 71% YoY to $1.8B; analysts project $4.27B for FY2026. Passes strongly.
- Balance sheet: Net cash position (~$8.6B cash and investments). Passes.
- Durable demand: Surgery volumes grow with aging populations; robotics share of total procedures is still small. Passes.

**Valuation attractiveness:** At $393.59 (2026-09-19), ISRG trades at a PE of ~44x — a premium, not cheap. However, the stock has pulled back 35% from its 52-week high of $603.88 reached in January 2025. If the analyst can confirm FCF continues compounding, the question becomes whether ~44x is justified by growth. This is a business the analyst should price carefully. Source: [Intuitive Surgical stock data via Robinhood/CNN](https://robinhood.com/us/en/stocks/ISRG/), [GuruFocus FCF](https://www.gurufocus.com/term/total-free-cash-flow/ISRG), [Globe and Mail FCF note](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/stocks/BSX-N/pressreleases/4223317/intuitive-surgical-s-record-cash-flow-gives-it-an-innovation-advantage/).

**Sector gap filled:** Medical devices — our health-care coverage (ABBV, JNJ, LLY, UNH) is entirely pharma/insurance. A high-quality medical device company with a recurring-revenue model is genuinely different.

### 3. INTU — Intuit Inc.

**One line on the business:** Intuit runs TurboTax (the dominant consumer tax-filing platform in the US, ~37%+ e-file share), QuickBooks (dominant SMB accounting software), Credit Karma (personal finance), and Mailchimp (SMB marketing) — all products millions of consumers and small businesses use every year.

**One line on the moat:** Switching costs are extremely high: a small business's entire financial history, payroll records, and client data live in QuickBooks; moving means re-entering years of data. TurboTax users return to the same product year after year because their prior-year return is pre-loaded and guides the interview. These are not network effects — they are pure switching-cost locks. Source: [Intuit Investor Day materials](https://investors.intuit.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1271/intuit-hosts-investor-day-reaffirms-first-quarter-and-fiscal-2026-guidance).

**Smell test:**
- Large and liquid: market cap ~$97.7B. Passes.
- Profitable: FY2026 revenue ~$21.3B (+13-14% YoY), GAAP EPS $15.49-$15.69 (+13-15% YoY), Q4 2026 EPS of $4.03 beat consensus of $3.29 by 22%. Passes strongly.
- Positive FCF: TTM FCF $7.715B as of April 2026, growing 26% YoY, 28.7% per-share FCF growth. Passes very strongly.
- Balance sheet: Software business with minimal capex; no heavy debt needed. (Full balance sheet to be confirmed in thesis.)
- Durable demand: Taxes are filed every year regardless of the economy. SMB accounting doesn't go away. Passes.

**Valuation attractiveness:** This is the most interesting valuation observation this week. INTU's trailing PE is ~20.8x and forward PE is ~14x (as of early September 2026) — 58% below its own historical average and 38% below the technology sector average. GuruFocus marks the forward PE as "significantly undervalued." For a company generating $7.7B of free cash flow and growing it at 26%, a 14x forward PE is notable. The analyst should investigate whether this is a pricing anomaly or a sign of structural concern (e.g., IRS free-file risk). Source: [GuruFocus forward PE](https://www.gurufocus.com/term/forward-pe-ratio/INTU), [StockAnalysis INTU statistics](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/intu/statistics/), [Intuit Q3 2026 press release](https://investors.intuit.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1312/intuit-reports-strong-third-quarter-results-and-raises-full-year-revenue-guidance).

**Sector gap filled:** Consumer-facing, switching-cost software. CRM and ADBE target large enterprises. INTU is the only name we'd be considering that serves consumers and small businesses with near-captive renewal rates.
