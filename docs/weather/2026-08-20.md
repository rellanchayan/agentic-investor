## The key terms (plain English)

- **SPY**: a fund holding the 500 largest US companies. It is our benchmark. SPY closed
  yesterday at 767.28.
- **200-day average**: the average SPY closing price over the last ~200 trading days. SPY's
  200-day average is 707.13. Today's price (767.28) is 8.5% above it, which tells us the
  broad market is in a technical uptrend — prices have been rising, not falling.
- **Interest rates / the Fed**: the Federal Reserve's target rate is 3.50-3.75%. This is
  elevated compared to the near-zero rates of 2020-2022. Higher rates mean borrowing costs
  more and future corporate profits are worth less today, which puts pressure on valuations.
- **Forward P/E**: the price of the S&P 500 divided by the next 12 months of expected earnings.
  Currently ~20x. The 10-year average is ~19x and the 5-year average is ~19.9x. Being above
  both means stocks are slightly expensive relative to recent history.
- **Shiller CAPE**: a longer-term valuation measure using 10 years of inflation-adjusted
  earnings. At 42.2, it is near historical highs. Readings above 40 have historically been
  followed by below-average long-term returns.
- **FOMC minutes**: the written record of the Federal Reserve's July 28-29 meeting discussion.
  These were released yesterday afternoon (Aug 19) at 2pm ET — that uncertainty is now resolved.
