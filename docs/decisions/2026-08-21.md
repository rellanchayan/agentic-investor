**The test a name must pass to be bought today:**
1. Conviction ≥ **4** (on a 1–5 scale), and
2. Margin of safety ≥ **18%** — i.e. the price must be at least 18% *below* our base fair value

3. It must have a valid, non-stale thesis, and
4. There must be room under the caps (we have all 8 slots free, so room is not the constraint), and
5. Budget must remain (it does — the full $5,000).

means we would be paying *more* than the business is worth to us.
