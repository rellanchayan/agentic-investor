## Decision: HOLD — no trades today

I am buying nothing today, and I am comfortable with that. The portfolio is entirely in cash, and
the honest reason is simple: none of the three businesses we have researched and are allowed to buy
is anywhere near a sensible price. Apple trades 57% above what we think it is worth, Microsoft 31%
above, and Visa 24% above — Visa is not merely above our base estimate, it is above even the *high*
end of our fair-value range. On top of that, today's posture is cautious, which means we demand an
18% discount to base fair value rather than the usual 12%, so the bar is higher than normal at
exactly the moment prices are higher than normal. The broad market itself is extended and expensive,
volatility is low (so nobody is panic-selling us anything cheap), the Fed is leaning hawkish, and
two high-impact data points — the jobs report on Friday Aug 7 and CPI on Aug 12 — land inside the
next eight trading days. Every other name on the watchlist has **no thesis**, and our rules are
absolute on that point: no thesis, no buy. Paying up for a good business in an expensive market is
the most common way patient investors quietly lose money, so the $5,000 stays unspent and the
$1,000,000 stays in cash. A missed buy costs nothing; an overpriced buy costs real money for years.
