## 3. The test each candidate had to pass

A BUY requires **all** of the following:

1. **Conviction ≥ 4** (on a 1-5 scale) — from `conviction_buy_threshold` in `state/config.json`.
2. **Margin of safety ≥ 18%.** Base requirement is `min_margin_of_safety` = 12%, plus the
   `cautious_mos_bump` = 6% because today's posture is cautious. So: **12% + 6% = 18%**.
3. **Room to own it** — its sector holds fewer than 3 names, and we hold fewer than 8 names.
4. **Budget remaining** — yes, $5,000.
5. **A valid, non-stale thesis** — checked; all three pass (`code/thesis.py --stale` reports
   `stale: []`, `invalid: []`).

Margin of safety is simply: **(base fair value − price) ÷ base fair value.** A *positive* number
means we are buying at a discount. A *negative* number means we would be paying a premium.

Conditions 3, 4 and 5 are satisfied for all three names. **Every one of them fails on price
(condition 2), and AAPL fails on conviction (condition 1) as well.**

every name is deeply overvalued is not a conservative posture — **it is a capability gap wearing
the costume of discipline.** We are not choosing to hold cash; we have no researched alternative

   **2026-08-14**; there is no file for Aug 17-21 or Aug 24. That is a gap of roughly six trading
   days. Per the playbook lesson of **2026-08-03**, after a gap of five or more missed reviews the
   fair-value estimates themselves — not just the price check — must be re-verified before being
