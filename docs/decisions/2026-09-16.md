## The honest cost of doing nothing

Our playbook carries a measured lesson worth repeating on a day like this: at 73 days with zero fills,
cash drag had cost roughly **2.49 percentage points versus SPY** — about 1 pp per month at the pace
SPY has run since inception. Sitting in cash is not free, and pretending otherwise would be dishonest.

But the fix for that is **not** lowering the bar today. Buying MSFT 26% above fair value to feel busy
converts a known, bounded cost (cash drag) into an unbounded one (permanent capital loss). The real
fix is the one the playbook already names: **expand the thesis bench**. A bench of three names, all
deeply overvalued, is a capability gap, not a strategy. That is research work for the weekend cycle,
not a reason to trade this morning.

Of the 29 names on our watchlist, **26 have no thesis at all** — GOOGL, NVDA, AVGO, ADBE, CRM, MA, JPM,
COST, WMT, PG, KO, PEP, HD, MCD, NKE, UNH, JNJ, LLY, ABBV, CAT, HON, UNP, XOM, CVX, DIS and SPY. Some
of these may well be cheap. We cannot know, and **no thesis means no buy** — that is a hard rule, not a
preference. Several are in sectors we have zero exposure to (health care, energy, industrials,
consumer staples) where valuations are typically far less stretched than in technology. That is where
research effort should go.
