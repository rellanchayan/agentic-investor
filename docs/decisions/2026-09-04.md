## The test a buy had to pass today

Our normal rule is to buy only at a **margin of safety** of 12% or more — meaning we pay at least 12% less than our estimate of the business's fair value. A cautious posture adds 6 percentage points, so today's bar was **an 18% discount to base fair value**. (The market-weather analyst independently recommended the same 18% minimum.)

Margin of safety = (base fair value − price) ÷ base fair value. A negative number means we would be *overpaying*.
