## What I did and why

**I placed no trades.** There are no buys, adds, trims, or sells. Since the portfolio is 100% cash with zero holdings, there is nothing to trim or sell — a trim or a sell requires owning something first. So the only live question this morning was: *is anything cheap enough to buy?* The answer is a clear no.

### The rule I applied

Our settings say buy only when a name is at least **12%** below our estimate of what the business is worth (`min_margin_of_safety` = 0.12), and only when conviction is **4 or higher** (`conviction_buy_threshold` = 4). Because today's posture is cautious, we add the `cautious_mos_bump` of **6 percentage points**, so this morning the bar is a **18% discount to base fair value**.

"Margin of safety" is just the cushion between what we would pay and what we think the business is worth: `(fair value − price) ÷ fair value`. A positive number means the stock is on sale. A negative number means we would be paying *more* than we think it is worth.
