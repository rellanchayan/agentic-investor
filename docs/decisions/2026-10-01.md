## The bar a buy had to clear today

These settings come from `state/config.json`:

1. **Conviction of at least 4** out of 5 (`conviction_buy_threshold`).
2. **Margin of safety of at least 18%.** That is the normal 12% (`min_margin_of_safety`) plus the 6% cautious bump (`cautious_mos_bump`), because today's posture is cautious.
3. A **valid thesis that is not stale.** `code/thesis.py --stale` lists all seven theses as fresh and none as invalid: AAPL, COST, GOOGL, MCD, MSFT, NKE and V.
4. Room in the portfolio. This is not a constraint today: we hold 0 of 8 names and have no sector exposure.

Margin of safety (MoS) = (base fair value − price) ÷ base fair value. A positive MoS means the stock trades below our estimate of what it is worth. A negative MoS means we would be paying more than we think it is worth.

A name must pass **all** of these tests to be bought. Today, none does.
