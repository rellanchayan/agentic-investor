### The bar we have to clear today

Our normal rule is: only buy a name we are at least **4/5 confident** in, and only when the price is
at least **12% below** our estimate of what the business is worth (that discount is the "margin of
safety" — our cushion for being wrong). Because today's posture is **cautious**, the config adds a
**+6% bump**, so this morning the required discount is **18%**.

All three are above the *top* of their fair-value bands, not merely above the midpoint. There is no
"exceptional reason" argument to make here, and buying into a Jackson Hole week at a 10-36% premium
to our own valuation would be the exact mistake our playbook warns about: *a missed buy costs
nothing; an overpriced buy costs money.*
