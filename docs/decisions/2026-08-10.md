## An honest flag: our fair values may themselves be out of date

Three things need to be said plainly rather than buried.

**1. We missed 11 consecutive trading days of morning review.** The last decision file on record
before this one is `docs/decisions/2026-07-23.md`. No decision file exists for Jul 24, 27, 28, 29,
30, 31, or Aug 3, 4, 5, 6, 7. Per the playbook lesson of 2026-06-22, those days are **process
failures, not hold decisions**. Whether any of MSFT, V or AAPL touched a buyable price during that
window cannot be recovered from the record. It probably did not — the gaps are enormous — but
"probably" is not the same as "checked."

**2. All three theses predate the most recent earnings season.** Every thesis on file was retrieved
**2026-06-16** with fundamentals as of FY2025 / 2026-Q2. Our own V thesis lists "Q3 FY2026 earnings
(expected late July 2026)" as a catalyst — that report fell inside our blackout window and has not
been read into the thesis. Microsoft's June-quarter fiscal year-end and Apple's fiscal Q3 follow
the same late-July / early-August cadence. **We have not verified what any of them reported.** The
playbook lesson of 2026-08-03 is explicit: after a gap of five or more missed reviews, re-verify the
underlying business assumptions before using a thesis to set an entry price — a price check alone
is not enough. Today's write-up is a price check. It is sufficient to justify *not buying*; it
would not be sufficient to justify buying.

**3. The size of the gap between price and our fair value is itself information.** AAPL trading 57%
above our base estimate, and MSFT 35% above, is a large enough divergence that one of two things is
true: the market is exuberant, or our valuations are too conservative and stale. I do not know
which, and it is not my job to decide — but note that the honest response to that uncertainty is
**not to buy**. If our numbers are right, these are overpriced. If our numbers are wrong, we do not
currently know what the right numbers are. Both roads lead to waiting.

**Action item for the research cycle (not for me today):** refresh all three theses against the
latest filings before the next attempted entry, and begin building theses on unresearched
watchlist names so the portfolio has more than three possible ideas.
