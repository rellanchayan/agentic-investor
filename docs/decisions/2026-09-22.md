## Decision for each ticker

### AAPL — WATCH (no action)
Conviction 3, below our threshold of 4. At $341.65 it trades 46.7% above our $232.82 base fair
value and 23.4% above the **high** end ($276.76). Thesis is filing-backed and current (refresh due
2026-10-17); the business is not broken — company-wide gross margin is 48.7% TTM against a 44%
break trigger, and TTM free cash flow is $136.7B against a $90B trigger. Nothing is wrong with
Apple. The price is simply far beyond anything we would pay. **Not a buy at any price near today's.**

### MSFT — WATCH (no action)
Conviction 3, below threshold. At $497.74 it is 24.5% above the $399.67 base fair value and 8.1%
above the $460.24 high end. The underlying business is performing well — Azure guidance for Q1
FY2027 is 44–45% constant-currency growth against a break trigger of sub-20%, and FY2026 operating
margin was 46.8% against a 38% trigger. Thesis current to 2026-10-17. **Excellent business, wrong
price.**

### GOOGL — WATCH (no action)
Conviction 3, below threshold. The most extreme gap on the board: $361.78 versus a $214.32 base
fair value, 68.8% overvalued and 40.5% above the $257.47 high end. The thesis itself flags a real
complication worth remembering — Alphabet is mid capital-spending shock, with free cash flow down
from $73.3B in FY2025 to roughly zero in the first half of 2026, the buyback ended, and an $80B
equity raise plus doubled debt. Headline earnings look flattering partly because $135.8B of
six-month pre-tax "profit" is an unrealised paper mark-up rather than cash. **Would need to more
than halve before we are interested.**

### V — WATCH (no action) — the closest thing to a candidate, and still far away
The **only** name that clears the conviction gate at 4. That is why it gets the most attention. It
still fails badly on price: $364.50 against a $291.73 base fair value is 24.9% overvalued, and it
is 7.2% above the $339.97 high end. Worth flagging honestly: V is **up** from the $355.29 in this
morning's context file, so it got further from our entry, not closer, while I was working. The
moat is intact — a near-duopoly with Mastercard controlling roughly 77% of the US credit-card
market, with Q3 FY2026 GAAP operating margin of 59.1% against a 55% break trigger and net
debt/EBITDA around 0.35x against a 2.0x trigger. **Right business, wrong price by about a third.**

### NKE — WATCH, and the weakest of the five (no action)
Conviction 2 — our lowest — and a moat we deliberately rate **narrow**, not wide. At $36.34 it is
11.5% above the $32.60 base fair value. It is the only name trading below its high end ($40.49,
−10.3%), which might tempt someone into calling it cheap. It is not. This is currently a shrinking

longer covers the $2.4B dividend, and Greater China has declined for eight consecutive quarters.
Low conviction plus a deteriorating fundamental trend plus no discount is an easy pass. **A
twelve-year-low share price is not the same thing as a bargain.**
