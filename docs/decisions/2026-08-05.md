## The one number that decides today

Our config says `min_margin_of_safety = 0.12`. The posture is **cautious**, which adds
`cautious_mos_bump = 0.06`.

> **Required margin of safety today = 12% + 6% = 18% below base fair value.**

Margin of safety = (base fair value − current price) ÷ base fair value. A *negative* number
means we would be paying **more** than we think the business is worth. Today all three of our
researched names are negative — badly so.
