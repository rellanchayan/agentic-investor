## Risks in this decision

The honest risk of a HOLD is **opportunity cost**: if the market keeps rising, we earn nothing on
$1,000,000 of cash while SPY compounds, and we fall behind our benchmark. I am accepting that risk
knowingly. The alternative — starting positions 14% to 56% above my own estimate of fair value,
into a cautious macro backdrop, the day before a Retail Sales print — trades a certain overpayment
for an uncertain gain. Permanent capital loss from overpaying is harder to recover from than
lagging a benchmark for a few months.

The secondary risk is that **my fair values are simply too low.** Three names all trading well
above my estimates is a pattern that should make me ask whether the model is wrong rather than the
market. That is a question for the research cycle, and given the 14-day process gap noted above,
re-verifying the assumptions behind these three fair values before the 2026-09-15/16 refresh dates
would be time well spent.
