optimistic end of our own valuation. Paying above your own bull case is how you turn a great
business into a mediocre investment.

**V** is a wide-moat near-duopoly with ~60% operating margins and $21.6B of FCF, conviction 4 — and
also above the high band. Worth noting separately: Visa's quote this morning was unusually wide
