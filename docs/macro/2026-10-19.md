average. That means the market has been trending up over the past year. An uptrend does not
make stocks cheap — SPY itself is near record highs — but it does confirm there is no
broad-market breakdown underway.

## Why Rates and Inflation Matter Here

When the 10-year US Treasury yield stands near 5.28%, a risk-free government bond earns more
than many stocks do in annual earnings yield. The S&P 500 at a 19-20x forward P/E implies an
earnings yield of roughly 5.0-5.3% — essentially no premium for the extra risk of owning
equities. That means stocks need to either fall in price or grow earnings substantially before
they offer a genuine discount to Treasuries. Until that gap widens, we demand a meaningful
margin of safety on every buy.
