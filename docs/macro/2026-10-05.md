# Market Weather — Monday, October 5, 2026

## Posture: CAUTIOUS

**One-line reason:** The market is in an uptrend but valuations are stretched (Shiller CAPE 41.07), the 10-year Treasury yield is elevated at 5.287%, and two major risk events land this week and next (Fed minutes Wednesday, CPI October 14) that could push rates higher or surprise in either direction.

---

## What the key terms mean today

**SPY** (the benchmark for all 500 largest US companies) sits at **772.37**, which is **7.1% above its 200-day average of 720.91**. That means the broad market is in a confirmed uptrend — prices have been rising for months. A market above its 200-day average is generally healthy territory.

**10-year Treasury yield** is at **5.287%**. This matters because when bonds pay 5.3%, investors demand a higher return from stocks too. A high yield puts downward pressure on stock valuations — especially growth stocks where profits are further in the future.

**The Fed** last raised its benchmark rate to **4.00%** in September. The odds of another hike at the October 28 meeting are only about 17% — markets mostly expect a hold. But the Fed's September meeting minutes release Wednesday will show exactly how divided officials were.

**Shiller CAPE ratio** (a long-term valuation measure using 10 years of inflation-adjusted earnings) is **41.07**, which is 25.6% above its long-term average of ~32.7. This is expensive by historical standards. It does not mean a crash is coming, but it does mean the market is pricing in a lot of good news already.
