## The market right now (plain English)

**SPY (our benchmark):** $764.26, which is 7.9% above its 200-day average of $708.39. Being above the 200-day average means the broad market trend is up — not a warning sign by itself. But trend and value are two different things, and the value signal is flashing yellow.

**Is the market expensive or cheap?** Expensive. The S&P 500's price-to-earnings ratio sits somewhere between 26x and 30x depending on the source (GuruFocus reports 26.09x as of Aug 21; trailing estimates from other sources reach 29-30x). The long-run historical average is roughly 18-20x. The Shiller CAPE — a longer-term smoothed version of P/E — is around 39x, which is in the top tier of historical readings. None of this means a crash is coming tomorrow, but it does mean that paying a full price for a stock today leaves little cushion if earnings disappoint.

**Interest rates:** The Federal Reserve last moved rates at its June 2026 meeting and has held steady at 3.50-3.75%. Rates at this level make borrowing more expensive for companies and make bonds a real alternative to stocks. Inflation is still running above the Fed's 2% target per the Fed's own June statement, which means there is no clear path to rate cuts yet. This is a headwind for high-multiple stocks.

**Pre-market futures:** S&P 500 futures are up ~0.4% and Nasdaq 100 futures up ~0.8% this morning. Markets are pointing modestly higher at the open, partly in anticipation of NVIDIA's earnings tomorrow night.
