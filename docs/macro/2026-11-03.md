## The Federal Reserve

The Fed held its benchmark interest rate at 3.75%-4.00% at the October 27-28 meeting, confirmed October 28, 2026. The vote was 10-2. Two officials (Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack) dissented, preferring an immediate quarter-point hike.

This matters because higher interest rates make future corporate earnings worth less in today's dollars, which pushes stock prices down. The Fed restarted its hiking cycle in September 2026 after a long pause. With two members openly pushing for more now, and December as a live decision, we are in a rising-rate environment. That historically compresses valuations — the opposite of what you want when names are already expensive.

**No FOMC meeting this week. Next meeting: December 8-9, 2026.**
