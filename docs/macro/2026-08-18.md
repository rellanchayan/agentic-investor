## SPY and the 200-day average

SPY (our benchmark fund that holds the 500 largest US companies) closed yesterday at **$768.38**. Its 200-day average price is **$706.26**.

Being above the 200-day average means the broad market is in an uptrend — not in distress. That is mildly positive. But the gap is wide: SPY is **8.8% above** that average, meaning the market has already rallied a long way. Distance above the average tends to make new entries riskier, not safer.

## Our three thesis candidates vs. our fair-value estimates

A "margin of safety" is the gap between what a stock costs and what we think it is worth. Positive = on sale; negative = too expensive.

| Ticker | Price today | Our base fair value | Margin of safety |
|---|---|---|---|
| AAPL | $310.96 | $195 | -59% (far too expensive) |
| MSFT | $480.81 | $378 | -27% (too expensive) |
| V | $362.38 | $297 | -22% (too expensive) |

None of our candidates are anywhere near a buy threshold. The negative margins of safety mean each stock costs significantly more than we estimate it to be worth today.

## What this means for us today

**Hold all cash. Do not buy anything.**

- The market is in an uptrend but prices are stretched.
- All three thesis stocks are trading at premiums to our fair-value estimates — they are not on sale.

This is deliberate, written patience — not passive inaction. We are watching prices, and we will act when a quality company crosses our buy threshold. Today is not that day.
