The weakness is being driven by two specific things that happened over the weekend and this morning
(described below).

---

## Big events this week — pay attention to these

### 1. FOMC rate decision — Wednesday, September 16 at 2:00pm ET (THE dominant event)

The Federal Reserve ("the Fed") sets the interest rate that ripples through all borrowing costs
in the economy. **Higher rates generally push stock prices lower; lower rates push them higher.**

The current target range is **3.50% to 3.75%**, where it has sat since December 2025. At the
July 28-29 meeting, the Fed voted 9-3 to hold that range.

At this meeting, markets are debating whether the Fed will:
- **Hold** — leave rates unchanged at 3.50-3.75%, OR
- **Hike 25 basis points** — raise rates to 3.75-4.00%

Markets are currently leaning toward a hike being more likely than not, driven by:
- A hawkish (rate-hike-favoring) speech from Fed Chair Warsh at Jackson Hole on August 28
- A solid August jobs report
- Inflation still running above the Fed's 2% goal (both August CPI and PPI came in warm)

This is a genuinely uncertain decision. A hike would likely cause stocks to fall further; a hold
would likely be a relief. The outcome matters enough that acting with capital before Wednesday's
2pm announcement would be premature.

### 2. No new CPI or jobs data this week

The calendar is quiet on data releases. The Aug CPI and PPI have already been released (both
warm), so there are no fresh inflation numbers to watch this week. All eyes are on the Fed.

---

## What else is moving markets this morning

### AI industry slowdown call (sector headwind for tech)

Over the weekend, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei and OpenAI CEO Sam Altman both made public
statements calling for a deliberate slowdown in the pace of frontier AI development, citing
safety concerns. Elon Musk also backed the view.

This is hitting AI and semiconductor stocks hard in pre-market trading:
- Nvidia: down ~3%
- Arm Holdings and Marvell Technology: down ~6% each
- Asian chip and AI-linked stocks (SK Hynix, Samsung, SoftBank) fell 4-10% overnight

This is a sector-specific story, not a collapse in the overall market. But because tech and AI
names make up a very large share of the S&P 500, their weakness pulls SPY down with them. There
is also uncertainty about whether Anthropic's IPO plans (targeting Nasdaq this autumn) will
proceed as expected.

### Oil spike

Brent crude oil is up roughly 3% this morning to about $107.65 per barrel, after Saudi Arabia
shut a key pipeline. Higher oil prices act like a tax on the economy and also keep inflation
elevated — which is another reason the Fed might choose to hike rather than hold on Wednesday.

## Is the market expensive or cheap right now?

The market is **modestly elevated**, not dangerously overpriced and not on sale:

- **Trailing P/E ratio: ~25.9** — this is in the upper-middle of the historical range (roughly
  normal to slightly expensive). It has actually come down 9% from earlier in 2026.
- **Forward P/E ratio: 19.1** — almost exactly in line with the 10-year average (19.0) and
  slightly below the 5-year average (19.8). On a forward basis, the market looks roughly fair.
- **VIX (fear gauge): ~15** in recent weeks — this is low, meaning investors have not been
  especially worried. Today's news may push it higher.

Summary: the market is not screaming "buy" or "run." It is roughly fairly valued on forward
earnings, but elevated enough that overpaying for individual names is a real risk.
