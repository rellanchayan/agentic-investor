# Market Weather — 2026-08-03

**Posture: CAUTIOUS**

## Macro Summary

The market is historically expensive and the macro backdrop offers little cover for new buys. SPY is 7.7% above its 200-day moving average (754.87 vs 700.82 SMA), which signals an intact uptrend — but valuations are stretched to near-record levels.

**Valuation:** Shiller CAPE ~41 (near second-highest on record; long-run median ~17). Trailing P/E ~28.8, forward P/E ~19.5. At these multiples, the margin of error on any purchase is minimal.

**Rates & Inflation:** The Fed held rates at 3.50–3.75% at the July 29 meeting, with three hawkish dissenters who wanted a hike. No rate cuts are expected; the next FOMC is September. CPI printed 3.5% annual (June), core 2.6% — both well above the 2% target. July CPI releases August 12.

**Today's market:** S&P 500 +1.12%, Nasdaq +1.66% mid-morning on geopolitical de-escalation (Trump calling off Iran attack) and oil prices falling. This is a genuine positive but doesn't change the valuation or rate picture.

**Earnings season:** Active — ~71% of S&P 500 has reported Q2. Major names this week: Palantir (today), AMD, Eli Lilly.
