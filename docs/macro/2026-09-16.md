## What the words mean (quick reference)

- **SPY** is a fund holding the 500 largest US companies — our benchmark. "The market" = SPY.
- **200-day average** is SPY's average closing price over the last ~200 trading days. Being above it signals an uptrend; below it signals a downtrend.
- **FOMC / the Fed** is the US central bank committee that sets interest rates. Higher rates make borrowing costlier and push stock prices down, because future profits are worth less when discounted at a higher rate.
- **CAPE (Shiller PE)** is a long-run valuation measure. A high number means stocks are expensive relative to their historical earnings.
- **Dot plot** is the Fed's published chart of where each member expects rates to go over the next few years. A hawkish (higher) dot plot is a headwind for stocks.
