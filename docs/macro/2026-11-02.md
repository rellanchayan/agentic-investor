# Market Weather — 2026-11-02

**Posture: CAUTIOUS**

The broad market is in a gentle uptrend — SPY (the S&P 500 fund, our benchmark) closed at 762.33, which is 4.6% above its 200-day average of 729.18. Being above that average tells us the market has been climbing over the past year, not falling apart. That part is fine.

The problem is price. The S&P 500's forward price-to-earnings ratio is roughly 19-20x, against a long-run historical norm of about 18.7x, and the trailing (backward-looking) multiple is in the mid-20s. In plain language: the overall market is mildly expensive relative to what companies actually earn. That is not a crash warning — it just means buyers are paying a full price, and patient investors should demand a larger discount before adding new money.

Interest rates are steady. The Federal Reserve held its most recent meeting on October 27-28; the next rate decision is not until December 9. No rate shock is coming this week.

## What this means for us today

Posture is **cautious**. The uptrend is intact, no rate emergency is in sight, but the market is paying full prices and AAPL's earnings tonight will add short-term noise to tech stocks. Under a cautious posture we demand a bigger discount than usual before buying. In practice, that bar is already miles away: all seven thesis candidates are trading 8-47% above our base fair-value estimates, so no buy is possible under any posture setting today. Hold cash. Watch AAPL earnings tonight for any large move that might affect our thesis fair-value assumptions.
