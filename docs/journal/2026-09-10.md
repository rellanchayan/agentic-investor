## Lessons

1. **A falling market is not a substitute for process.** The gap vs SPY narrowed today because
   SPY fell, not because we did anything right. If the market recovers tomorrow, the gap widens
   again. The only durable way to close the gap is to run the morning review, find something
   priced within our fair-value range, and buy it. Passive cash-holding is not a strategy — it
   is drift.

2. **The morning review gap is now four weeks old.** The last decision log with live price checks
   was Aug 26. Fourteen more trading days have passed with no systematic review. All three
   thesis names remain far above fair value, so no buy would have cleared anyway — but we do not
   know that from analysis, we are inferring it from today's closing prices. The review needs to
   run every trading morning so the record is complete, not reconstructed in hindsight.

3. **The bench needs new names.** Every journal since Aug 26 has noted that health care, consumer
   staples, industrials, and energy have no thesis coverage. The thesis list (AAPL, MSFT, V)
   covers only technology and payments. A quality business in one of the uncovered sectors,
   priced more reasonably than our current names, may be available right now — we cannot know
   until the weekly research runs and theses are written. This is the single most actionable item.
