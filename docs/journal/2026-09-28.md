## Lessons

1. **SPY retreating 0.78 pp in a day is a reminder that the gap fluctuates, but does not fix
   the underlying problem.** Our -1.99 pp gap today looks better than Friday's -2.77 pp, but
   that is entirely because SPY fell, not because we did anything right. Cash does not compound.
   The gap will widen again the next time SPY rallies. The only durable fix is to hold good
   businesses bought at sensible prices. That requires a wider bench with theses, not a smaller
   gap due to a down day.

2. **MCD is the one name within realistic striking distance, but it needs two things to be right
   simultaneously: a 17% price drop AND a conviction upgrade to 4.** Both conditions need to be
   true on the same morning. If MCD falls 17% but the thesis still scores conviction 3, we
   cannot buy. The thesis refresh schedule should treat MCD as a priority — understanding what
   would lift conviction from 3 to 4 is work we can do now, before a price drop. Then we would
   be ready, rather than scrambling.

3. **The coming week has four macro catalysts that could create price dislocations:** JOLTS on
   Tuesday Sep 29, PCE inflation on Thursday Oct 1, the jobs report on Friday Oct 2, and the
   NKE thesis refresh on Oct 5. Each one could push prices toward or away from entry points.
   The morning review must run on all four of those days without exception. Missing a morning
   review on a high-volatility day is the most likely way we miss an entry opportunity.
