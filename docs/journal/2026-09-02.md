## Lessons

1. **The SPY gap is growing every day we sit still.** The deficit moved from -1.47 pp yesterday
   to -1.94 pp today — 0.47 pp of additional drag in a single session. Patience in the face of
   overvalued prices is correct discipline. But we are not writing morning reviews, not expanding
   the thesis bench, and not pricing any new names. That is not patience; it is inactivity. The
   distinction matters.

2. **The thesis bench needs new names urgently.** We have three theses on file, all for stocks
   trading above their fair value. If none of them clear our hurdle, we need to research names
   that might. Sectors we have not covered — health care, consumer staples, industrials, energy —
   were flagged as overdue more than a week ago and remain untouched. Every day without a
   buyable name is a day the budget goes undeployed.

3. **A morning review costs almost nothing and preserves optionality.** Writing "we looked and
   passed" takes minutes. Not writing it means that on the day prices briefly dip into range,
   we may not be watching. The daily review is not bureaucracy — it is the mechanism that catches
   the opportunity when it briefly appears.
