## Lessons

1. **Cash drag is not free.** Every week of all-cash while SPY rises costs roughly 0.06 percentage points of relative performance. That is not catastrophic, but it compounds. The remedy is a wider thesis bench, not a lower buy standard.

2. **The bench is too narrow.** Seven theses is not enough coverage. With seven names, all currently overpriced by our own math, there is no action to take no matter how disciplined the process. Research on additional quality names (MA, KO, PG, JNJ, and others from the 26-name watchlist) is the highest-priority work between now and the next event date (FOMC minutes, Oct 7).

3. **"Nothing" can be the right answer, but it needs to be earned, not default.** Today's no-action was justified by honest price analysis. But the pattern of no-action is widening. The next journal should note whether thesis coverage has grown, not just repeat that nothing was bought.
