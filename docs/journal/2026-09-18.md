## Lessons

1. **A narrowing SPY gap is not a compliment when it comes from SPY falling, not us buying
   well.** Today the gap went from -1.61 pp to -1.47 pp. The temptation is to read that as
   progress. It is not. We contributed nothing. On any day SPY rallies, the gap will widen again
   by the same mechanical logic. Progress will come only when we own something that earns a real
   return — and we own nothing. The honest read of today: 95 days at inception-level equity, 0.00%
   return, 100% cash.

2. **Triple witching day required no special action.** Elevated options-expiration volume and
   intraday volatility are a known quarterly event. The correct response for a long-term investor
   with LIMIT + DAY orders is to do exactly what we did: set targets at thesis-derived levels and
   let the session run. There is no edge in adjusting entry prices to exploit triple witching
   gyrations on names we have no reason to believe are near fair value.

3. **Saturday weekly-research is now the critical bottleneck.** The bench has held three names
   for months. All three are overvalued. The only way out of 95+ days of 0.00% return is to find
   names that are at or near fair value, or to wait for prices to fall. Expanding the bench from
   3 to 8+ quality candidates is not optional — it is the minimum work required before we can
   deploy the $5,000 daily budget. If Saturday's research does not produce at least two new
   serious thesis candidates, the morning reviews will continue to produce the same empty result.
