## Lessons

1. **A narrowing gap is not a vindication.** Our gap to SPY closed from 380 bps to 310 bps only because SPY fell slightly — not because we did anything right. Cash-drag is the expected cost of waiting, and it will resume the moment the market recovers. We should not interpret a two-day pullback as confirmation that patience is paying off; that conclusion requires years of data, not two days of red.

2. **The process improvement from Oct 6 held.** Rebuilding context after the thesis refresh, not before, is the correct ordering. The decision file explicitly confirms the timestamp sequence. Small process fixes should be noted when they work, not just when they fail — that is how we know the learning is real.

3. **V and MCD remain the only actionable names in the watchlist, for different reasons.** V has the conviction (4) but is priced 36% above its cautious buy gate ($239.22). MCD has the price proximity (17% from its cautious gate of $195.45) but lacks the conviction (3, not 4) and needs a research upgrade before price even becomes the binding constraint. The upcoming October 14 CPI print and October 28 FOMC decision are the next potential catalysts for a move toward those gates. We should have the MCD conviction question settled before then.
