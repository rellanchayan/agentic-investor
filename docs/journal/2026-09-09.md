## Lessons

1. **The SPY gap narrowing today due to a market pullback is not a reprieve.** When SPY dips,
   we look relatively better only because we held cash. The moment the market recovers, the gap
   widens again. Cash is not a hedge strategy here — it is an empty portfolio. The gap closes only
   by owning quality businesses bought at fair prices. That requires the morning review to run.

2. **Ten trading days without a live price check means the Aug 26 thesis-entry thresholds are
   potentially stale.** If AAPL, MSFT, or V corrected during this window, we may have missed an
   entry. We cannot know. If any of the three fell toward fair value and then recovered, the
   opportunity is gone and unrecorded. This is the same failure mode the playbook documented on
   2026-08-03 after the July gap — it has happened again.

3. **Writing the same lesson four journals in a row without a change in outcome is evidence the
   lesson format is not the bottleneck.** The playbook already contains the permanent rule. The
   morning cycle is not running. Repeating the observation here is honest record-keeping, but it is
   not a solution. The actual fix — automating or externally triggering the morning cycle — has been
   named in the playbook since Aug 21 and has not been implemented.
