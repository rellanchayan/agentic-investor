## Lessons

1. **Patience has a measurable price, and we accept it.** We are 2.89 pp behind SPY with no positions and no fills since inception. That is not hidden — it is the honest cost of requiring a margin of safety. If every quality name stays above fair value, we will keep trailing. The answer is a wider thesis bench (more names researched), not a lower bar.

2. **Macro events raise the entry bar, not lower it.** FOMC day + pending GDP/PCE data = the cautious bump from 12% to 18% was correct. A fill that barely passes the normal bar on a high-uncertainty day is more likely to look expensive in hindsight. Waiting for the data is free when there is nothing close to fair value anyway.

3. **Refresh theses before deciding, even on hold days.** V's base fair value jumped 5.2% (from $291.73 to $307.01) after the mid-morning refresh using FY2026 actuals. An older number would have shown a slightly less expensive entry target. The process of rebuilding context after a thesis refresh (per the 2026-10-06 playbook lesson) caught this. Keep doing it.
