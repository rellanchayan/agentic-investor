## Lessons

1. **A missed morning review on a flagged macro day is a process failure, not a neutral event.**
   The 2026-09-28 journal named PCE on Oct 1 as one of four sessions where the morning cycle
   must run without exception. It did not run. Whether or not today's prices offered an entry
   point is now unknowable. The cost may be zero — but we cannot know, and that is the problem.
   A process designed to be ready for dislocations only works if it runs.

2. **107 days, zero fills, -1.80 pp behind SPY.** The gap has improved marginally from last
   Thursday's reading, but the underlying reality is unchanged: an account that never acts cannot
   compound. Patient investing requires price discipline, but it also requires a bench of
   researched names with current theses and a morning review that actually runs. Research work
   on the 26 watchlist names with no thesis remains the single highest-leverage task.

3. **The SPY gap fluctuation (-2.77 pp on Sep 25, -1.99 pp on Sep 28, -1.80 pp today) is noise,
   not signal.** Each narrowing happened because SPY fell, not because we did anything productive.
   Tracking the gap week to week is fine; treating a narrowing caused by an index pullback as
   progress would be dishonest.
