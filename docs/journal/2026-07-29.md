## Lessons

1. **Four consecutive days without a decision file is a structural failure, not a run of bad
   luck.** The playbook has now recorded this exact pattern four times: 2026-06-17/18,
   2026-06-22, 2026-07-14, and now 2026-07-24 through 2026-07-29. The rule exists in writing.
   The rule is violated repeatedly. Until the morning cycle is enforced mechanically — not
   voluntarily — the journal will keep recording this same failure. The journal writer can
   document it; only the system can fix it.

2. **Today is FOMC day and we have no record of the market context or our posture response.**
   The 2026-07-23 decision file flagged the July 28–29 FOMC meeting as a key event. No
   decision file exists for either day. Whatever the FOMC announced, and whatever that meant
   for yields, valuations, and the cautious posture we were carrying, is unrecorded. Future
   thesis reviews will not be able to reconstruct the macro backdrop for this week. That is a
   permanent gap in the audit trail.

3. **The "lead over SPY" growing to 2.80 pp is a warning sign, not a win.** The wider our
   cash-vs-SPY gap grows, the more pressure there is — psychological and statistical — to
   eventually enter the market after a drawdown, when prices may already be recovering. Buying
   into a relief rally because "we missed the bottom while sitting on cash" is the exact trap
   patient investors should avoid. The goal is to buy when a quality business is cheap relative
   to its intrinsic value, not when the market has recently been falling. Keeping this
   distinction sharp requires a morning review every single day.
