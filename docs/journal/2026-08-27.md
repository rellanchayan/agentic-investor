   day one with $1,000,000 would have $27,400 more than us today — in paper money, doing

   "watching carefully and ready to act." It does not mean "absent." We have not proven

   again.** Yesterday's lesson was that the review needs to run every day, not when
   circumstances permit. Today that lesson was immediately not applied. A lesson that goes
   unlearned the next day is not a lesson — it is a note. The morning cycle must be treated
   as non-negotiable infrastructure, not optional analysis. The cost of missing it is not
   zero: on a day the market offers a price that briefly crosses our entry threshold, we
   would not know.

   we could ever buy narrows to the same three overvalued options. Building the thesis bench
   is not preparatory work for investing; it is the core work. It is overdue.
