## Lessons

**1. The gap vs SPY compounds quietly.**
We are down 1.57 points vs SPY after 106 days, purely from being in cash. The morning file noted this costs roughly 1 percentage point per month. That estimate is tracking well. The answer is not to lower the quality bar — it is to expand the bench of researched names so that when a name does come into range, we have already done the work. The playbook is correct; the execution gap is the number of names with finished theses.

**2. A soft PCE print does not make an expensive stock cheap.**
The August PCE was the best macro news in months. The correct response was to note it in the posture and leave the price targets unchanged. We did that. Macro data points are inputs to the interest-rate picture, not direct inputs to individual stock fair values. Conflating them — lowering the quality bar on a good CPI day — would be a mistake.

**3. MCD conviction review is the most urgent pre-work.**
MCD is the closest name to a buy (price needs a 16% decline to reach the cautious entry of $195.45, and conviction needs to rise to 4). That review must be finished before the price drops, not after. The jobs report on Fri Oct 2 and the NKE thesis refresh on Mon Oct 5 are the next scheduled events. MCD conviction work should happen in parallel, not in response to a price move.
