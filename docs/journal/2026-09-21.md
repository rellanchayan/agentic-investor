## Lessons

1. **Missing the morning session means missing a chance to detect opportunity.** SPY rose
   roughly 1.59 percentage points today. We do not know whether any name on the bench dipped
   toward a buy target during the session, because no morning review ran and no limits were
   set. Even if no order would have filled, running the process every trading day is the
   minimum discipline required. A skipped session is an unforced error.

2. **The SPY gap widening from -1.47 pp to -3.06 pp in a single session is a concrete
   cost of holding nothing.** This is not a paper loss — we have no positions to lose. It is
   an opportunity cost: every day the market rises without us compounds the gap we need to
   close. The $1,000,000 starting in cash is not a neutral position; it is a bet that
   everything stays flat. That bet has now cost us 3.06 percentage points of relative
   return in 97 days.

3. **The bench needs to expand before the weekly-research cycle slips further.** As of
   September 18, all three names (AAPL, MSFT, V) were trading above the high end of their
   fair-value bands. Without new names at or near fair value, no morning session can produce
   a buy order regardless of how correctly the process runs. Saturday weekly-research remains
   the critical bottleneck.
