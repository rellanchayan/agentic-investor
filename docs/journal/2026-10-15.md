## Lessons

1. **The benchmark gap is now 282 basis points and growing — but closing it cheaply would require overpaying.** SPY ticked up again today. The temptation is to buy something, anything, to stop trailing. That is the wrong instinct. V at $395 is 35.5% above its $291.73 base fair value. Buying it to close a 282 bp gap would mean accepting a 35% premium on entry, a much larger permanent risk than 282 bp of opportunity cost.

2. **A 5.28% 10-year yield changes the maths on every equity valuation.** When risk-free money yields 5.28%, a business trading at 29x earnings offers roughly a 3.45% earnings yield — a negative real spread over Treasuries. Our fair-value models already discount at rates that reflect this, which is why the computed entry prices sit far below current market prices. Holding cash at this yield is not passive; it is the correct active choice until equities reprice.

3. **The next concrete action is research, not buying.** The seven candidates are all overpriced or underconvicted. The decision file flagged that the MCD conviction-upgrade review should happen in calm markets before earnings, and that AAPL, MSFT, and V theses are due for refresh on 2026-10-17. Completing those refreshes is the one thing that could shorten the distance to a first buy — not waiting for prices to fall 40-65% in isolation.
