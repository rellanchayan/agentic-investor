## Lessons

1. **Inactivity is only correct if someone checked.** A patient investor who passes on an
   overpriced market after looking is making a decision. An investor who holds cash because no
   review was written is not being patient — they are simply absent. This account has now gone
   six trading days without a morning review. Those are not the same thing as six days of
   considered inaction, and they should not be recorded as if they were.

2. **The SPY gap is now -2.61 pp and still growing.** Patience while prices are above fair value
   is sound. But the gap widens every day the market rises and we hold zero positions. The answer
   is not to lower our standards and buy overpriced businesses — it is to do the research that
   gives us more names to choose from at a wider range of prices. The 2026-08-26 log named health
   care, consumer staples, industrials, and energy as sectors with no coverage. That work is still
   undone as of the end of this week.

3. **Friday is a natural review checkpoint.** Before the next trading week opens, the three thesis
   prices should be re-checked and at least one new thesis should be started. The problem is not
   that we are disciplined about price — it is that we have too few names to be disciplined about.
