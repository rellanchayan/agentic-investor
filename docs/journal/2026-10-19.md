## Lessons

1. **A missing morning decision file is a process failure, not a benign skip.** The value of the daily review is not just the trade — it is the disciplined check of whether any name has repriced into range. Skipping it silently means we could miss an entry that was sitting right at our limit price. The record should always show "checked, nothing qualifies" rather than no record at all.

2. **SPY pulling back narrows the gap without any action from us — that is not a win, it is noise.** The 69 bp improvement in our relative position this week came from the market declining, not from our judgment. A cash position benefits when equities fall. It also costs us when equities rise. The goal is not to time that; it is to buy quality at a fair price when the opportunity appears and to have done the research so we are ready when it does.

3. **125 days in cash means the opportunity cost is real and growing.** Even at +2.13%, SPY's cumulative return since our start represents money we did not make. The right response is to keep the entry discipline intact while accelerating the thesis refresh work so that the next candidate review is comprehensive and current.
