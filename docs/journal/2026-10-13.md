1. **A deliberate, documented HOLD is not the same as inaction — it is a decision.** Today we produced a full candidate review, evaluated seven names against two independent gates, and concluded that none were buyable. That conclusion is written down, with numbers, in `docs/decisions/2026-10-13.md`. This contrasts with yesterday (Oct 12), which had no decision file. Showing up and saying "nothing passes" explicitly is more valuable than simply not trading.

2. **CPI tomorrow is a real test of patience, not a buy trigger on its own.** A cool September CPI print might send SPY higher and make the gap feel worse. A hot print might pull prices lower and close the distance to our entry levels slightly. Neither outcome changes the gates: V would still need to fall from $391 to around $239 even in a bad market scenario. The lesson is to watch catalysts as context, not as excuses to move the price gates.

3. **Seven theses, zero buyable names: the research backlog is the real constraint.** The decision memo acknowledges this plainly. The five names beyond V and MCD all require a conviction upgrade before they can be bought at any price. The only way to break the cash-holding pattern is to complete that conviction work — or to research new names that are already cheap. Waiting for current prices to fall 40-65% is not a credible near-term plan. Expanding the thesis pipeline is.
