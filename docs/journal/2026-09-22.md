## Lessons

1. **Missing process days are a real cost, even when no trades result.** It is tempting to view
   skipped reviews on overvalued names as harmless — we were not going to buy anything anyway.
   But the review is also how we notice when prices move sharply toward fair value, when news
   breaks that changes a thesis, or when a new name worth adding to the bench appears. Two days
   without a review (09-21 and 09-22) means two days of blind exposure. The discipline of the
   daily cycle is the point, not just the trades it occasionally produces.

2. **A widening SPY gap while holding 100% cash is mathematically unavoidable in a rising
   market.** SPY is up +3.05% since inception. We are at +0.00%. The gap will keep widening on
   every day SPY finishes positive and we hold no positions. The only path to closing this gap
   is to own quality businesses bought at sensible prices. Until the bench contains names trading
   near fair value, or until prices correct toward our entry targets, the gap will compound.

3. **98 days of $0 return is data, not a holding pattern.** The weekly-research cycle was
   identified as the critical bottleneck on 2026-09-18. If that work has not expanded the bench
   beyond AAPL, MSFT, and V, the morning reviews will continue to produce nothing. The next
   available action is either Saturday weekly-research or waiting for a price correction in the
   existing bench names. Noting this plainly so the learning-coach can assess whether the research
   backlog is being addressed.
