   1.47 pp on Tuesday. We trail by 3.00 pp today. The arithmetic of missing a sharp two-day rally
   is not subtle — it shows up immediately and permanently in the since-inception return. Patience
   when prices are above fair value is correct. Patience combined with six consecutive days of no
   morning review is not patience — it is absence. The account needs to be watched even when the
   answer is "pass."

   history.** The 2026-08-28 journal named the missing review as a concrete failure. The 2026-09-01
   journal repeated that. Today is the third journal in a row to record the same problem. Naming the
   gap in a journal is not a substitute for fixing it. The morning review must run automatically and
   produce a written file every trading day — including days when the result is "nothing to do."

3. **No new theses have been built since 2026-08-26, and possibly longer.** The binding constraint
   on this account is bench coverage: three names (AAPL, MSFT, V), all deeply above fair value, with
   no alternatives ready to act on when prices eventually soften. Every week that passes without new

   2026-08-26. It remains overdue as of today.
