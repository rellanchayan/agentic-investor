## Lessons

1. **The gap between us and SPY is now +2.03 pp and widening again.** After a brief narrowing
   when SPY dipped last Friday, the benchmark recovered today and the trailing gap is back above
   2 pp. Cash does not beat an equity benchmark over time. The pattern since June 16 is
   unambiguous: SPY goes up, we do nothing, the gap grows. Reversals in SPY temporarily narrow
   the gap, which is not a form of outperformance.

2. **Seven trading days without a morning review is a process failure, not a strategy.** A
   deliberate, documented decision to hold cash would appear in `docs/decisions/`. Silence
   does not equal a decision. The CLAUDE.md daily cycle calls for a morning review every
   trading day. Running that review — even if it concludes "hold, no action today" — is the
   minimum process requirement. Going seven consecutive days without it means any price move
   toward a thesis buy target during those days went unseen and unacted on.

3. **On the next trading day, the morning review must produce a written decision, even if the
   decision is explicitly to hold.** Theses for AAPL, MSFT, and V are on file. Prices move
   every day. A review that compares current price to the thesis buy target takes minutes and
   produces a record. That record is the difference between a disciplined hold and inaction.
